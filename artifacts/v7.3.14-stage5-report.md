## Pre-change reproduction

`artifacts/v7.3.14-stage5-prechange-characterization.json` was generated before production editing. It proves:

- Hypergraph mode selected `V2V NOT_REQUESTED`, produced `v2vEdges = []`, and therefore had zero topology springs and zero co-membership visual lines.
- Force physics ran only at `verts.length <= 700`; Reheat raised alpha but could not make a 701-vertex graph tick.
- every computed Line Graph edge was walked on every frame, and `draw()` unconditionally requested another RAF after settled/static rendering;
- ring hover geometry existed but non-node mouse-down started pan rather than selecting a ring;
- numeric zero could be lost through the `find(...) || find(...)` chain;
- weight zero was hidden by three truthiness checks;
- canvas backing dimensions were based on one `offsetWidth` measurement and fixed height without ResizeObserver.

The characterization also records that Stage 1 had already protected Map position storage and literal `"null"` presence semantics. Those were preserved instead of misreported as new failures.

## Preview architecture after Stage 5

Three concepts now have independent contracts:

1. Hypergraph Force topology uses the Stage 3 incidence index and O(I) member-to-hyperedge-centroid attraction. It never requests or stores a V2V clique.
2. Hypergraph co-membership is a Preview-only visual overlay. It is complete under the Preview budget or deterministically fair-share sampled by hyperedge, and its status is visible to users.
3. Line Graph remains the exact Stage 4 bounded analytical projection. Preview applies a separate deterministic 4,000-line LOD cap without changing analytical/export data.

Small graphs use exact pairwise repulsion. Graphs above 700 use deterministic sampled repulsion with at most 24 samples per vertex. The boundary changes algorithmic strategy, not whether physics works. Force attraction remains O(I) in both modes. The configured constants are centralized in `src/visualization/hypergraphLayout.js`.

## Interaction, metadata, and lifecycle

- Vertex hit testing has first priority; Hypergraph ring perimeter testing has second priority; otherwise mouse-down pans/clears.
- Drawing, hover, and click share `computeHyperedgeBounds()`; overlapping rings select nearest perimeter, then smaller ring, then display order.
- Search uses explicit `undefined` detection, so numeric/canonical zero works and literal `"null"` stays distinct from unset state.
- Position storage remains Map-backed through initialization, merge, force, drawing, drag, hover, and hit testing.
- Weight 0 appears in hover, selected metadata, and the hyperedge list.
- ResizeObserver updates CSS/backing dimensions independently; DPR 1 and 2 are verified and absurd DPR is capped at 3. Resize does not reset world positions, pan, zoom, selection, layout, or simulation energy.
- The RAF scheduler prevents duplicate loops, stops after Force settles or interaction ends, restarts on Reheat/interaction, preserves the final frame, and cancels outstanding work on cleanup.
