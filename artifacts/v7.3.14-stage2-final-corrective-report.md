## Corrections

- The quote-aware lexical scanner is now the sole authority for full-line CSV comments. CSV, Incidence, CSR/CSC, and Auto Detect remove blank masked rows but do not discard decoded data merely because its first cell starts with `#`.
- Strict `parseCsvDocument()` remains comment-unaware and unchanged.
- CSV serialization quotes a first-column data value beginning with `#`. Incidence hyperedge ID `#h1` therefore serializes as `"#h1",...` and round-trips through Auto Detect and Incidence parsing.
- Quoted leading-space variants, real comments, quoted `# comment` data, inline `#`, multiline quoted data, numeric zero, literal `null`, and prototype-like identifiers are covered by the final regression.
- `expH2HAvailabilityResult()` represents computed, identifier-unrepresentable, over-budget, and not-requested availability explicitly.
- The App's H2H descriptor is never nullable. Non-computed states keep explanatory preview/copy text while the existing guarded download path returns `ok:false` before invoking the downloader and does not return a filename.
- The corrective does not request or force H2H projection computation.
